THE LITTLE THINGS, EXPLAINED

A few helpful answers.

No rush. Start with what's on your mind.

How do I play?

Fit pieces onto the 8 × 8 board. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and make space. Place all three pieces in your set to receive three more.

Drag a piece into position, or tap a piece and then tap the board where its top-left corner should go. There is no timer.

What's the difference between Classic and Daily?

Classic is a personal high-score challenge with a fresh opening each time you start. Daily ritual begins with the same pieces for the same local date. Your placements affect which pieces follow, so different choices can produce different runs.

Daily scores are personal records on your device. There is no global leaderboard or multiplayer competition.

How does undo work?

You can undo your latest move once per set of three pieces. That restores the board, score, and piece. After you use the undo, finish that set before another undo becomes available in the next set.

An invalid placement does not use your undo. Leaving and reopening the game does not reset the allowance.

How do I save or continue a game?

Your current run, preferences, and personal bests save locally. Use the back arrow to return home, then choose Continue game to resume an eligible run.

The app keeps one current run. Starting a fresh board replaces it after confirmation when applicable. An old Daily run is replaced when the local date changes.

Why has my Daily board changed?

Daily follows your device's local date. A new day brings a new opening, and an unfinished board from a previous day expires. Changing your device date or time zone can also change which day the game uses.

Why is a piece dimmed, or why did my run end?

A dimmed piece does not currently fit. Another piece may still fit and clear room for it. Your run ends only when none of the remaining pieces can be placed. If an undo is available, you can take back the last move.

Why can't I hear sounds or feel vibration?

Check Soft sounds and Gentle vibration in Settings, along with your device volume. On iPhone, game sounds respect silent mode. Vibration is available only on supported hardware.

Can I play offline? Are there ads or purchases?

The installed game can be played offline. You do not need an account. All included gameplay is available without ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Can I recover progress after deleting the app?

Luma Blocks has no account-based save service. Deleting the installed app removes its local data; offloading an app may keep its data. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup and may restore it later, depending on your settings.

Support cannot retrieve a saved board from a Luma Blocks server. See the privacy policy for more about local data and backups.
