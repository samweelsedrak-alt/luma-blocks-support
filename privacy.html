01 / YOUR GAME DATA

Your progress stays in the game's local storage.

Luma Blocks saves the information it needs to let you continue playing:

  • Your current board, available pieces, score, cleared lines, moves, streak, undo state, game mode, and Adventure level.
  • Classic and Daily personal best scores, plus the date used for Daily play.
  • Adventure stars and unlocked levels, the dates of earned Daily badges, and your selected block colours. Chapter trophies are determined from your saved level progress.
  • Your background music, sound-effect, and vibration preferences and whether you have viewed the instructions.

This information is stored on your device. Luma Blocks does not operate an account system, remote gameplay service, or its own cloud save service. Daily puzzles use your device's local date. Music and sound effects are bundled with the app; they do not require streaming or microphone recording.

✦

The current game has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking features. It does not upload gameplay records to a Luma Blocks server.

Our intended audience includes children, teenagers and adults. Gameplay does not ask for a player's name, email address or age, and there is no player messaging. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support on their behalf. Parents or guardians can use the support address for privacy questions or to request deletion of correspondence involving a child.

02 / SUPPORT EMAIL

You choose what to share when you ask for help.

If you contact samweel.sedrak@gmail.com, the email address you send from, your message, and any attachments you include are received by the support contact. These details are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue you describe.

Email is sent and stored through your email provider and the recipient's email provider. Their privacy practices and retention rules also apply. The app does not automatically attach your saved board or device information.

Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox. You can email the same address to request deletion of correspondence you have sent. Email providers manage their own backup and retention systems.

03 / GOOGLE PLAY TESTING

Google Play testing is a separate Google service.

If you join an early-access or beta test through Google Play, Google collects and shares certain device and app-usage information and user-triggered events with the developer. Feedback you submit through Play is also available to the developer. We use those reports to improve the game; Google manages its service records under its own privacy practices. The game itself does not automatically send support messages or upload saved progress. See Google's testing information.

04 / YOUR CHOICES & BACKUPS

Local saves and device backups are different.

You can change music, sound effects, and vibration preferences in the game's Settings. Starting a new board replaces the current run; it does not erase personal best scores, earned rewards, or preferences.

Deleting the installed app removes its local game data. Clearing the app's storage on Android also removes local records. Offloading an app on iPhone or iPad may retain its data. Copies may remain in device or cloud backups, and restoring a backup may restore game data.

Your operating system and backup provider control whether app data is backed up, restored, or retained. Manage those copies through your device and backup settings. Luma Blocks does not control those systems or offer a separate account-based backup.

Apple explains these controls in What does iCloud back up? and Manage your iCloud storage.

On Android, app data may be included in device backups and restored depending on your Android version, device, app support and backup settings. Google or your device provider manages those copies. See Google's Android backup guide.

05 / THIS WEBSITE

A simple support site.

This website contains no tracking scripts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, forms, or embedded third-party content. The site's HTML and CSS do not set cookies or use browser storage. Its styles and images are served with the site.

When this website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed into GitHub. This hosting data is separate from the locally saved game data. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Email links open your email app. If you choose an external help link, the destination website's privacy practices apply.

06 / CHANGES & CONTACT

Questions are welcome.

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the policy version.

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact samweel.sedrak@gmail.com.
